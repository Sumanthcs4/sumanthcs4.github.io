About me

I’m Sumanth, an AI engineer based in Bengaluru. I like taking an idea from a notebook to an application someone can try.

I earned my master’s in Data Science and Analytics at Jain University. In my personal projects, I’ve explored how language models find evidence, how to compare the answers they give, and how to turn a trained model into a usable prediction service.

One question I keep coming back to is whether a more complex approach earns its extra time and cost. That’s what led me to build RAG‑Comparator and test three retrieval methods on the same questions.

Featured projects

RAG‑Comparator

I built a dashboard to compare three ways to search research papers. Across 20 evaluation questions, reranking improved answer quality as judged by a language model. Average response time rose from 2.73 to 3.47 seconds.

Medical question answering

I fine-tuned Llama 2 7B on medical questions and answers, achieving a BERTScore F1 of 0.798 on the test split. A separate retrieval step finds relevant passages from more than 25 medical PDFs in under a second.
